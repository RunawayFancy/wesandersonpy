# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Contributors should communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, accept responsibility for mistakes, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact channel once the public repository is configured. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action while protecting reporter privacy as far as possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), accessed 2026-08-23. If this summary conflicts with the Contributor Covenant, the linked version is the interpretive reference.
